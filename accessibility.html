Accessibility Statement

Access to emotional wellness information should not be limited by physical, cognitive, or technological barriers.

Our Commitment

The Guided Pathway Foundation is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to as many people as possible, including individuals with disabilities. We believe that access to emotional wellness information and resources should not be limited by physical, cognitive, or technological barriers.

What We Are Working Toward

We are actively improving the accessibility of our site and content on an ongoing basis, including:

  • Adding descriptive alt text to all images across the site.
  • Ensuring sufficient color contrast throughout all pages.
  • Structuring page content with clear headings and readable font sizes.
  • Reviewing all forms and interactive elements for keyboard accessibility.
  • Working toward conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA as our standard.

Known Limitations

As a newly established nonprofit organization, we acknowledge that our site does not yet fully meet all accessibility standards. We are committed to addressing these gaps as resources allow and as our site continues to develop.

Feedback and Assistance

If you experience any difficulty accessing content on our website or need information provided in an alternative format, please contact us. We take accessibility feedback seriously and will make every effort to accommodate your needs.
