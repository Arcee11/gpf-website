Past Events

Wellness Community Market · October 3, 2026

The Guided Pathway Foundation joined the Wellness Community Market at Bottega Exchange in Las Vegas, hosted by Her Alchemy and I Am A Mom's Mom as part of the 6 Weeks of Wellness collection. We spent Saturday morning sharing the Lantern Project with the many families that stopped by our table, and guests took home our free self help emotional intelligence guide along with a few treats and a smile.

Women's Leadership Conference · September 2026

The Guided Pathway Foundation was a Promotional Gift Bag Sponsor at the Chamber of Commerce of Cape Coral's 15th Annual Women's Leadership Conference, placing 150 Lantern Project magnets into attendee gift bags.

A Million Steps for Scleroderma, Las Vegas · August 29, 2026

The Guided Pathway Foundation joined A Million Steps for Scleroderma at Meadows Mall in Las Vegas as a community organization and sponsor. More than 80 participants walked and the event raised $17,532 for scleroderma research and support. Our team distributed around 100 Lantern Project pathway magnets, putting free emotional intelligence tools into the hands of families across the community.

Our raffle winner with her prize basket at The Zone Nutrition.

Meet a Famous Mouse and Community Fundraiser · July 18, 2026

The Guided Pathway Foundation teamed up with The Zone Nutrition at 2612 Santa Barbara Boulevard South in Cape Coral for a morning of family fun in support of the Lantern Project. Guests met a famous mouse, posed for photos, and joined a community raffle, with prizes sponsored by Culver's and Cooter's Cans.

Thank you to everyone who came out, took a photo, and gave. Every dollar raised helps place free emotional intelligence tools into more hands across our community.
