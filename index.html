Emotional Intelligence Can Be Taught. That Changes Everything.

The numbers show the cost of waiting: 40% of U.S. high school students reported persistent feelings of sadness or hopelessness in 2023 and 1 in 5 adults has been diagnosed with depression or an anxiety disorder.

Research is clear that people with stronger emotional intelligence experience better mental health, stronger relationships and greater life satisfaction. It can be learned, practiced and passed from one person to another.

We exist for the space before the storm. The quiet moment when a tool, a conversation or a single resource can change the trajectory. That is where prevention lives and that is where we work.
