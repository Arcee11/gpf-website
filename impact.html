What the numbers mean

Every Number Is a Family

About 475 families have received emotional intelligence tools from The Guided Pathway Foundation so far. Roughly 200 of those families are in Las Vegas, Nevada. 75 are in Orlando, Florida. The remainder are spread across Southwest Florida, including about 210 families reached through our SWFL Community Reach Program.

These are not impressions or clicks. They are real people: parents who picked up a book at a community event, families who placed a Lantern Project magnet on their fridge, students whose teachers put a resource in their hands. Each one now has a practical tool for understanding and expressing emotion, available in the quiet moment before a hard one.

Where we are headed

From 475 Families to 50,000 Homes

Our forward goal is clear: emotional wellness materials reaching 50,000 homes across our eight Southwest Florida communities, Cape Coral, Fort Myers, Fort Myers Beach, Babcock Ranch, Estero, Bonita Springs, Naples, and Marco Island, alongside continued growth in Las Vegas, Nevada.

Getting there means scaling what already works: growing the Lantern Project so more families can open free guided tools with one scan, deepening school partnerships that place resources directly with students and educators, and expanding our community distribution network one neighborhood at a time.

Prevention grows from an already good place. We are not waiting for crisis to arrive. We are building the tools, the access points, and the community strength now, and your support is what carries them further.
