Every Number Is a Family

About 475 families have received emotional intelligence tools from The Guided Pathway Foundation so far, across Las Vegas, Orlando, and Southwest Florida. This count runs through September 2026 and grows as new distributions are tallied.

These are not impressions or clicks. They are the 150 Lantern Project magnets placed in gift bags at the Women's Leadership Conference in Cape Coral, the roughly 100 magnets our team handed out at the Scleroderma walk in Las Vegas, and the families who stopped at our table at the Wellness Community Market and the July fundraiser at The Zone Nutrition. Each one now has a practical tool for understanding and expressing emotion, available in the quiet moment before a hard one.

From 475 Families to 50,000 Homes

Our goal: emotional wellness materials reaching 50,000 homes. That is the target behind the closing appeal on this page, and every distribution moves the count toward it.

Our forward goal is clear: emotional wellness materials reaching 50,000 homes across our eight Southwest Florida communities, Cape Coral, Fort Myers, Fort Myers Beach, Babcock Ranch, Estero, Bonita Springs, Naples, and Marco Island, alongside continued growth in Las Vegas, Nevada.
