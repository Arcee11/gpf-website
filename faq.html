What is The Guided Pathway Foundation?

The Guided Pathway Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to expanding access to emotional intelligence tools and prevention based wellness resources across communities and organizations. Our mission is to place books and resources developed from clinical frameworks directly into the hands of individuals, families, students, and professionals before emotional challenges deepen into crisis. We believe emotional intelligence is a fundamental life skill that every person deserves access to, regardless of income, background, or circumstance.

Is my donation tax deductible? What is your EIN?

Yes. The Guided Pathway Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) tax exempt nonprofit organization. Our Employer Identification Number is 41-4735497. Donations made to the foundation are tax deductible to the full extent permitted by law.

Where are you located, and what areas do you serve?

The Guided Pathway Foundation is based in Southwest Florida and Las Vegas, Nevada. Our first active distribution initiative serves multiple communities across the region including Cape Coral, Fort Myers, Fort Myers Beach, Babcock Ranch, Estero, Bonita Springs, Naples, Marco Island, and Las Vegas, Nevada. Our Organizational Wellness Distribution Program serves businesses, schools, nonprofits, and institutions nationwide. We are currently working on expanding distribution and tools across Florida and Nevada.

What is Give Energy Make Smiles LLC?

Give Energy Make Smiles LLC is a licensed clinical practice and wellness product company that serves as the clinical partner behind the tools and resources distributed through The Guided Pathway Foundation's programs. GEMS is led by Kayla Luu, LCSW, a licensed clinical social worker practicing in Florida and Arizona. The clinical frameworks, tools, and products developed within GEMS directly inform what the foundation distributes, ensuring that every resource placed into a community or organization is grounded in real therapeutic practice, not generic wellness content.

Who leads the foundation?

The Guided Pathway Foundation is led by Shawn Deshield, who serves as President of the foundation and Chief Executive Officer of Give Energy Make Smiles LLC. His leadership bridges the clinical expertise of GEMS with the community reach mission of GPF.

What is the Community Reach Program?

The Community Reach Program is a large scale book distribution initiative designed to place emotional intelligence resources informed by emotional wellness research directly into Southwest Florida and Las Vegas communities. The program distributes books through community events, neighborhood access points, schools, and local outreach efforts, reaching individuals, families, and youth where they already live, gather, and grow. The goal is to put practical emotional wellness tools into the hands of people who may never walk into a therapist's office but will engage with a resource placed in front of them.

What is the Organizational Wellness Distribution Program?

The Organizational Wellness Distribution Program is a structured bulk distribution model for businesses, schools, nonprofits, healthcare organizations, and other institutions that want to place emotional intelligence tools into the hands of the people they serve. Organizations purchase books in bulk and distribute them on site and as take home resources. The program operates across two lanes, on site organizational use and take home individual access, and is available to organizations nationwide.

What books do you distribute?

The books distributed through The Guided Pathway Foundation's programs are developed in partnership with Give Energy Make Smiles LLC and are shaped by clinical emotional intelligence frameworks created by a licensed mental health professional. They are designed to be practical, accessible, and engaging for a wide range of readers, from students and families to workplace teams and community members. Specific titles and product details are available in our shop.

What are your resources based on?

Our resources are informed by emotional wellness research and shaped with guidance from licensed professionals, including our clinical partner Give Energy Make Smiles. We do not distribute generic self help or motivational content. The tools we place into communities and organizations come from a solid research foundation. We are not a clinical provider and our resources are not therapy. They are practical tools for growth, resilience, and everyday emotional skill building.

How can I get involved?

There are several ways to get involved. You can donate to fund book distribution, purchase an organizational package through our shop, volunteer your time or resources, or reach out to explore a community partnership. If you are a local organization, school, business, or community group in Southwest Florida or Las Vegas, Nevada and want to serve as a distribution access point, contact us at info@theguidedpathwayfoundation.com.

What is in the store packages?

Each package in our shop includes a set of emotional intelligence books informed by emotional wellness research, intended for bulk distribution within an organization or community setting. Package sizes and pricing vary based on the level of distribution. Full details including what each package includes, who it is best suited for, and how it can be used are listed on each individual product page in our shop.

Who are the store packages for?

Our shop packages are designed primarily for individuals, organizations, schools, businesses, nonprofits, and community groups that want to distribute books at scale.

What happens after I purchase?

After your purchase is completed you will receive an order confirmation. Our team will follow up with you regarding delivery logistics, distribution guidance, and any support you need to get the books into the right hands. If you have specific distribution goals or need guidance on how to use the books most effectively within your organization, we are happy to help.

How long does fulfillment take?

Distribution packages are fulfilled within 45 to 90 days of purchase. Because these are bulk orders coordinated for organizational and community distribution, fulfillment requires additional preparation and logistics. Our team will follow up with you after your order is confirmed to provide delivery details and timeline updates.

Can I use HSA or FSA funds?

Eligibility for HSA or FSA use depends on the specific product and the guidelines of your benefits plan administrator. We recommend checking with your plan provider directly regarding whether emotional wellness resources qualify under your specific plan. If you need documentation to support a claim, contact us and we will provide what we can.

Will I receive a donation receipt?

Yes. The Guided Pathway Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization and your donation is tax deductible to the full extent permitted by law. You will receive an official donation receipt following your contribution. Our EIN is 41-4735497. Please retain your receipt for tax purposes.

What does the General Fund support?

Donations to the General Fund support the foundation's core operations including book purchasing, community distribution logistics, outreach efforts, and program development across our Southwest Florida and Las Vegas, Nevada service areas. Every dollar is directed toward expanding access to emotional intelligence tools for individuals, families, schools, and organizations. We are committed to keeping operational overhead lean so the majority of funds go directly toward distribution and programming.

Can I donate in honor or in memory of someone?

Yes. If you would like to make a donation in honor or in memory of someone, please include their name in the notes field at checkout or send us an email after your donation is complete. We will acknowledge it in your official receipt and can send a notification to a family member or designee if you provide their contact information.

How do I get my donation receipt?

An official 501(c)(3) donation receipt will be sent to the email address associated with your donation following your contribution. If you do not receive it within a reasonable time or need a replacement copy, contact us at info@theguidedpathwayfoundation.com and we will send one promptly.
